# Runbook: AI provider outage

**What happens by itself.** After 5 failed provider calls in a row (or >5 % errors over 60 s) the breaker opens: AI reports are planned and written with the
built-in layout and wording immediately (< 2 s), one probe goes to the provider every 30 s, and the first success closes the breaker. Users see
"The AI service is not answering right now" on the AI reports page; everything else keeps working.

**You do**
1. Check `GET /api/v1/ai/usage` (Settings → AI usage this month): failed calls and the status line (`provider_down`).
2. Check the provider's status page. If it is a short outage, do nothing.
3. If it lasts more than 15 minutes (alert to the on-call developer): decide whether to switch provider. Set `AI_PROVIDER` to another configured provider and
   restart the API and `worker-ai`. **Run the golden set on that provider first** (`pytest -m eval` with its key); do not switch to one that has not passed.
4. Last resort: `AI_ENABLED=false` (every company builtin within 60 s, no restart needed for the flag itself) or `AI_PROVIDER=none` (needs a restart).
5. After recovery, reports made with built-in wording can be regenerated by their owners with "Rewrite the text".
